Good to know

Who are these courses for?

Beginners and early-career learners who want practical AI and IT infrastructure skills. Each course clearly shows its level before you start.

How long do I keep access?

Your purchase includes lifetime access to the course materials, so you can return to the lessons and labs whenever you need them.

What do I need for the VMware labs?

A computer capable of running a virtual lab, enough free storage, and the software listed in the course setup lesson. Hardware requirements vary by lab.

Is there a certificate?

Each completed course includes a downloadable certificate of completion.
